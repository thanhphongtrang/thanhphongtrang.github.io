Doctoral teaching at IPKL will appear here as it happens. What is already true:

## Language tutor & instructional designer

**University of Education Language Center** · Ho Chi Minh City · 2017–2020

Taught adult language learners and designed the courses they took: ten courses
across proficiency levels, built on constructivist principles and aimed at
scaffolding learner autonomy rather than delivering content at people. When the
pandemic closed the classrooms, I led the migration to online delivery, which
meant discovering, at speed and in public, just how much of teaching is not the
material.

That period explains where my doctorate landed. The questions I now ask about
teacher educators and AI are ones I first met as a teacher with a curriculum, a
room, and a decision to make about what deserved my students' time.

## Analytics community of practice

**Volvo Cars** · Gothenburg · 2023–2026

Co-founded and ran an internal community of practice, coaching cross-functional
teams in data-driven methods. Teaching adults who never signed up for a course,
inside an organisation that measures things: a useful corrective to any romance
about how learning happens.

## Mentoring

I mentor early-career product managers and analysts through
[ADPList](https://adplist.org/mentors/phong-trang-tran-thanh), mostly around
analytics and AI product work. It is small-scale and steady, and it keeps me close
to how practitioners reason about their tools day to day.
