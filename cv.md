## Positions

### Doctoral researcher (*doktorand*) in education
**University of Gothenburg**, Department of Education, Communication and Learning
(IPKL) · From August 2026

In GRAITE, Sweden's national graduate school for AI in Teacher Education
(VR-funded, nine universities). Researching how teacher educators enact
professional judgment and agency when co-creating with generative AI.

### Product manager, GenAI
**Volvo Cars AB** · Gothenburg · 2023–2026

Led GenAI product work serving 100+ markets. Built AI that assists human
decisions: buyer search, customer care, analyst workflows. Ran product discovery
on behavioural analytics and experiments; drove roughly 10% uplift in online
sales conversion; cut cloud costs by about 80% and query latency by 25%.
Programme-managed European Accessibility Act compliance across 27 markets.
Co-founded the analytics community of practice. In 2024 ran an internal study on
epistemic ownership in AI-assisted coding (traces plus survey, n=127).

### Commercial learning & learning analytics project manager
**Volvo Cars USA** · New Jersey · 2022–2023

Commercial learning and learning analytics: the education thread and the data
thread running through one job, three years before the doctorate made that
pairing a research question.

### Earlier positions in analytics and data

- **Data analyst**, Incrementum AB · Gothenburg · 2021–2022. SQL and Python
  analysis, ETL pipelines supporting product decisions.
- **Passenger experience & data manager**, China Southern Airlines · Ho Chi Minh
  City · 2017–2020. Operational data systems for 500+ daily flights; cut passenger
  information discrepancies by 30%.

### Teaching

- **Language tutor & instructional designer**, University of Education Language
  Center · Ho Chi Minh City · 2017–2020. See [Teaching](/teaching/).
- **Mentor**, [ADPList](https://adplist.org/mentors/phong-trang-tran-thanh) ·
  ongoing. Early-career product managers and analysts.

## Education

**M.Sc. Applied IT and Learning**, University of Gothenburg · 2020–2022
Human-AI interaction, digital learning design, learning sciences. Thesis:
*Chatbot-Mediated Engagement in Learning*, graded **VG**. Funded by the Swedish
Institute scholarship below.

**M.Sc. Information Systems**, Linnaeus University · 2023–2024 (part-time)
Business analytics, big data analytics capability, AI and organisational trust.
The line of work behind the [ECIS 2026 paper](/publications/).

**B.A. Mandarin Language Education**, Education University, Ho Chi Minh City ·
2013–2017. Second language acquisition, learning theory, literacy identity.
