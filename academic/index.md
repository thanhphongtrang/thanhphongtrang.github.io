I am a doctoral researcher in GRAITE, Sweden's national graduate school for AI in
Teacher Education, studying how teacher educators keep professional judgment when
generative AI joins their work. This is the academic half of what I do.

- [Research](/academic/research/): the questions, and where they come from.
- [Publications](/academic/publications/): peer-reviewed work and other outputs.
- [Teaching](/academic/teaching/): from language classrooms to doctoral courses.
- [Talks](/academic/talks/): conference presentations and invited talks.
- [Artefacts](/academic/artefacts/): the research software I build for the studies.

The other half of the bridge: [Industry work](/industry/), the AI products I built
