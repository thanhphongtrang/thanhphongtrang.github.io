From August 2026 I am a doctoral researcher (*doktorand*) in GenAI and human-AI
interaction at the University of Gothenburg, at the Department of Education,
Communication and Learning (IPKL). My position belongs to **GRAITE**, Sweden's national graduate
school for AI in Teacher Education: a VR-funded collaboration across nine
universities.

## The questions

Plainly, without the apparatus:

- **What do teacher educators actually do when a model hands them something
  plausible?** Not what they say they did afterwards, but what happens in the
  moment. Where do they push back, where do they let it pass, and on what
  grounds?
- **Is professional judgment something you keep by exercising it?** If a capable
  model absorbs the routine parts of planning and drafting, the interesting
  question is not whether the output is good. It is what becomes of the judgment
  that used to be spent producing it.
- **What does teacher knowledge look like when part of the work is delegated?** I
  use **TPACK**, the braid of technological, pedagogical and content knowledge, as
  my frame: it is the vocabulary the field already argues in, and generative AI
  puts real strain on its joins.

Beneath all three sits *agency*. Not as a slogan, but as something you can watch a
person exercise, hedge, or quietly hand over.

## Why it matters beyond the seminar room

Teacher educators train the people who will teach everyone else's children. If
professional judgment quietly migrates into a model that a school did not choose,
cannot inspect, and does not own, the issue stops being one of craft and becomes
one of accountability: who answers for what gets taught?

## Where this comes from

This is not a swerve. In 2024, while building AI products at Volvo Cars, I ran an
internal study on epistemic ownership in AI-assisted coding, combining behavioural
traces of what people accepted, rejected and edited with survey data (n=127).

What stayed with me was methodological rather than any particular result.
**Asking people about their own reliance and observing what they actually do are
two different measurements**, and the gap between them is where the interesting
question lives. That is why I build my own instrument instead of borrowing one.
Judgment is precisely the thing that goes quiet in self-report; nobody remembers
the suggestion they let straight through. You have to watch the work.
